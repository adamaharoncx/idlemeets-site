Features

What Idle does

Find the meet, check the plan, post your own, show what you drive, and keep up with the people and groups you meet there.

Download on the App Store Find Meets Hosts Open Help Center

Idle for Android is in active development.

Discover meets

See the meet before you commit to the drive.

  • See local meets posted for now, tonight, and later. How to find meets in Idle.
  • Open the flyer, time, location, host, RSVP, parking notes, directions, and updates.
  • Public meets and group-only meets appear according to your access.

E85 & photo spots

Know where you are fueling and what you are mixing.

  • Look for nearby E85 stops, then confirm the station before driving. See the E85 finder.
  • Browse approved, community-rated places to photograph a car. See photo spots.
  • Recommend new photo spots and rate approved locations.
  • Use saved vehicle fuel details and the ethanol calculator for blend planning. Explore the calculator.

Host meets

Publish the details people will ask for.

  • Add the time, location, host credit, visibility, rules, and notes.
  • Create a flyer from the meet and share it without retyping the plan.
  • Add parking plans, ride-out details, and pre-meets when they help attendees.
  • Pick up local meet drafts and add an optional end time when a meet has a clear window.
  • Credit another host clearly when posting on their behalf.
  • Official Idle posts can be clearly attributed to Idle.

Host Tools

Change the plan without sending everyone hunting.

  • Review active, upcoming, and past hosted meets.
  • Post a host update when timing, weather, parking, or another detail changes.
  • Edit the flyer, parking guidance, ride-out plan, and supported meet details.
  • Use Host again to bring back a successful meet with a fresh future date.

Profiles & garage

Show the car behind the username.

  • Create a driver profile with your garage, socials, badges, and meet history.
  • Add cars or bikes with gallery photos and a primary vehicle.
  • Document modifications, owner-reported power, performance results, and optional photo proof where Build & Performance is available.
  • Crop garage photos and blur a license plate before saving when you want to.
  • Use eligible profile options such as supporter badges, photo rings, and profile card decor with Idle membership.
  • Choose whether your profile location appears.
  • Save fuel details for your build when useful.
  • Share your profile with an IdleCode. Explore garage profiles.

Pulse

Post the quick stuff.

  • Share short posts, photos, polls, comments, likes, and reposts. Explore Pulse.
  • Open Pulse photos full screen, zoom in, swipe them away, or save them to Photos.
  • Attach public meet cards so people can open the full plan.
  • Mention drivers and groups to keep the right people in the conversation.
  • Share useful links with a clear browser handoff.

Groups

Discussion for later. Chat for right now.

  • Create or join groups for crews, private requests, discussion, chat, and group-only meets. Explore groups.
  • Invite the right drivers and manage member roles with clear group tools.
  • Use group discussion for longer updates and group chat for quick coordination.
  • Mention group members when a chat message needs their attention.
  • Tune alerts for each group you belong to.

Messages & connections

Pick up where the meet left off.

  • Connect with drivers you know and coordinate with your circle.
  • Message accepted connections before and after the meet, with retry controls when a send fails.
  • Open shared photos full screen, zoom in, swipe them away, or save them to Photos.
  • Share a short status with connections when you are rolling out or looking for plans.
  • Send a Convoy invite to a connection from inside Idle.

Convoys

Share the drive with the people in it.

  • Start or join a short-lived Convoy with a code or link. Explore Convoys.
  • Choose whether to share live location while actively in the Convoy.
  • Keep active Convoy status visible with Live Activities where supported.

CarPlay

Upcoming meets, without the full app on the dash.

  • View eligible upcoming mapped meets when signed in on iPhone.
  • Open quick meet details from the CarPlay map.
  • Start Apple Maps directions from supported meet locations.

Widgets & Live Activities

Put the next plan where you will see it.

  • See your next public meet at a glance with an iPhone widget.
  • Keep meet-day details visible with Live Activities where supported.
  • Open the relevant meet or Convoy quickly from supported system surfaces.

Safety & privacy

Decide who sees what.

  • Choose what parts of your profile are visible.
  • Control notification, map, media, appearance, and account settings.
  • Review permissions when you want nearby meets, weather, reminders, and Activity alerts to work their best.
  • Report content and block accounts when needed.

Demo mode

Look around before signing up.

  • Explore sample meets, groups, Pulse, messages, garage content, and connections.
  • Use demo mode as a local preview of the app experience.
  • Switch to a real account when you are ready to use Idle with your own profile.

Looking for the steps?

The Help Center explains each feature. Email support for account access, safety, deletion, or anything private.
