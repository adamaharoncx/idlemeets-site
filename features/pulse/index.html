Community feed

Share the update, keep private plans private.

Pulse is for updates you are comfortable sharing with the community in Idle. Keep private, group-only, or invite-only plans inside the spaces where they belong.

01

Posts and photos

Post quick updates from the local scene.

  • Share short updates or photos when something useful is happening around the scene.
  • Comment, reply, like, or repost when a post is worth keeping in the feed.
  • If a photo or post fails, check the content and your connection, then try again.
02

Links and meet cards

Give people context without burying the plan.

  • Add useful web links when they help the post or comment.
  • Attach public meet cards where supported so people can open the full plan.
  • Keep private, group-only, or invite-only meets in the right space instead of posting them to Pulse.
03

Polls and mentions

Ask quick questions and bring in the right people.

  • Use polls for meet plans, routes, opinions, and local car-scene decisions.
  • Mention drivers or groups when they are relevant and visible to you.
  • A post may disappear if it is deleted, reported, hidden, blocked, or no longer available to you.

Questions

Pulse questions.

Short answers for drivers looking for a car-scene feed, quick meet updates, Pulse polls, or a cleaner way to share public meet context.

What is Idle Pulse?

Pulse is Idle's quick car-scene feed for short updates, photos, links, comments, likes, reposts, mentions, polls, and public meet cards.

Can I attach a meet to a Pulse post?

You can attach public meet cards where supported. Keep private, group-only, or invite-only meets in the spaces where they belong.

Can Pulse polls help with meet plans?

Yes. Pulse polls are quick questions for meet plans, routes, opinions, and local car-scene decisions. Current Pulse polls cannot be combined with photo posts or public meet cards.

Will every Pulse post stay visible?

Not always. A post may disappear if it is deleted, reported, hidden, blocked, or no longer available to you.
