Profile and garage

The username tells you who. The garage tells you what they brought.

Each vehicle gets its own details and photos, while your profile holds the bio, socials, badges, groups, and privacy choices around it.

01

Driver profile

Start with the person.

  • Keep your username, avatar, banner, bio, home base, socials, badges, groups, and meet context together.
  • Use eligible profile style options such as badges, profile photo rings, and profile card decor.
  • Choose whether your profile location appears.
  • Use your profile to give connections a better sense of who they met and what you drive.
  • Review profile visibility settings when you want more control over what details are shown.
02

Garage

Give each car or bike its own page.

  • Add vehicles with year, make, model, trim, and photos when available.
  • Choose a primary vehicle so the right badge can appear around the app next to your name.
  • Crop a garage photo and blur the license plate before saving when you want to.
03

Build & Performance

Record the number. Say where it came from.

  • Where available, add modifications, owner-reported power, and performance results.
  • Attach optional photo proof to support a build entry.
  • Performance figures are entered by the owner; they are not verified by Idle.
04

Fuel details

Fuel notes stay with the owner and the car.

  • Save fuel type, tank size, pump gas, ethanol defaults, and notes when they help your build.
  • Use saved vehicle details with Idle's ethanol calculator for faster blend planning.
  • Review your saved values before using them for fuel planning.

Questions

What appears on a garage profile

Vehicles, build details, privacy, profile style, and the difference between an owner's claim and Idle verification.

Can I build a garage profile in Idle?

Yes. Idle lets you create a driver profile, add cars or bikes to your garage, choose a primary vehicle, add photos, and keep useful profile details together.

Can I document modifications and performance details?

Where available, Build & Performance lets you add modifications, owner-reported power, performance results, and optional photo proof. These figures are not verified by Idle.

Can I save vehicle fuel details?

Yes. You can save useful vehicle fuel details for your own build, then use those details with Idle's fuel tools when planning ethanol blends.

Who can see my garage?

Your garage lives inside Idle. What people can see depends on your profile settings, your connections, and where you share your profile.

Can I control what profile details are visible?

Yes. Idle includes profile visibility controls, including whether your profile location appears.

Can I customize profile style?

Eligible profiles can use profile style options such as badges, profile photo rings, and profile card decor. Some options may require Idle membership.
