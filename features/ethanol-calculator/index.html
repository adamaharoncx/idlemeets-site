Questions

Ethanol calculator questions.

Short answers for drivers searching for an ethanol calculator, E85 calculator, or fuel blend calculator for their build.

What does Idle's ethanol calculator do?

Idle's ethanol calculator estimates blend amounts from the current fuel, current ethanol, target ethanol, fill amount, and vehicle defaults you enter.

Can I use saved vehicle fuel details?

Yes. You can save useful fuel details for your own garage vehicles, such as tank size, pump gas, ethanol defaults, and notes, then use them when planning a blend.

Does the calculator know what my car can safely run?

No. The calculator is a planning tool from your inputs. Only use blends your vehicle, tune, and parts support.

Does Idle guarantee E85 station availability?

No. Confirm station details, fuel availability, and hours before driving.
