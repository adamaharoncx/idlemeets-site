The calculation

Four inputs in. An estimate out.

Save recurring values to the vehicle, or enter them manually for a one-off fill. Review every number before you use the result.

01

Inputs

A wrong input makes a wrong estimate.

  • Enter current fuel amount, current ethanol, target ethanol, and fill amount.
  • Use manual values when you want to plan a one-off fill.
  • Review every value before using the result.
02

Garage defaults

Save the recurring numbers to the car.

  • Save tank size, pump gas, ethanol defaults, and notes for your own garage vehicles.
  • Update saved values when your setup, tune, or fuel plan changes.
  • Keep build-specific fuel notes with the vehicle they belong to.
03

Before you drive

Your setup decides what is safe. The calculator does not.

  • Confirm station details, fuel availability, and hours before driving.
  • Only use blends your vehicle, tune, and parts support.
  • When something looks off, check the numbers before filling up.

Questions

What the result means

The calculator handles the mix from your inputs. It does not inspect your tune, parts, or station fuel.

What does Idle's ethanol calculator do?

Idle's ethanol calculator estimates blend amounts from the current fuel, current ethanol, target ethanol, fill amount, and vehicle defaults you enter.

Can I use saved vehicle fuel details?

Yes. You can save useful fuel details for your own garage vehicles, such as tank size, pump gas, ethanol defaults, and notes, then use them when planning a blend.

Does the calculator know what my car can safely run?

No. The calculator is a planning tool from your inputs. Only use blends your vehicle, tune, and parts support.

Does Idle guarantee E85 station availability?

No. Confirm station details, fuel availability, and hours before driving.
