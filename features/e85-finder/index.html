Questions

E85 and fuel tool questions.

Clear answers for drivers searching for E85 near me, an ethanol calculator, or a fuel blend calculator built into their car meet app.

Can Idle help me find E85 near me?

Idle can help iPhone users look for nearby E85 stops when station data is available. Drivers should confirm station details, availability, and hours before driving.

Does Idle include an ethanol calculator?

Yes. Idle includes an ethanol calculator that estimates blend amounts from the current fuel, target ethanol, fill amount, and vehicle defaults you enter.

Can I save fuel details for my car?

Yes. You can save useful fuel details for your own garage vehicles, such as fuel type, tank size, pump gas, ethanol defaults, and notes.

Does Idle guarantee station availability or fuel compatibility?

No. E85 search and the ethanol calculator are planning tools. Confirm station details before driving and only use blends your vehicle, tune, and parts support.
