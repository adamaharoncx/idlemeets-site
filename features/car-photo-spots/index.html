Photo spots

Know where you are shooting before the cars arrive.

Before the shoot

A pin is not permission.

Check access, hours, signs, traffic, and property rules when you arrive. A location appearing in Idle does not make every parking space or angle available to use.

01

Find spots

Compare the backdrop before making the drive.

  • Browse approved spots for car photos or scouting near your discovery area.
  • Review the name, tags, rating, distance, and photos when they are available.
  • Open directions if you decide to go, then confirm the location works before shooting.
02

Recommend spots

Recommend what another driver would need to know.

  • Include a clear name, location or city, useful tags, and an optional photo.
  • New submissions may need review before they appear broadly in Idle.
  • If location services are not the right path, enter the location manually where the app allows it.
03

Rate approved spots

Rate the place you actually visited.

  • Rate approved spots when they are available to you.
  • Add a photo when it helps show the backdrop or current conditions.
  • Follow local rules, respect private property, and avoid unsafe behavior when visiting or recommending a spot.

Questions

Before you set up the shot

How spots get added, what ratings mean, and why local rules still come first.

Can Idle help me find car photo spots?

Yes. Idle helps iPhone users browse approved car photo spots near their discovery area when spots are available.

Can I recommend a new photo spot?

Yes. You can recommend a spot with a name, location or city, helpful tags, and an optional photo. New submissions may need review before they appear broadly.

Can I rate a photo spot?

You can rate approved spots or add a rating photo when the spot is available to you in Idle.

Does Idle list private or unsafe photo locations publicly?

Idle's public website does not publish private user location data or fake photo spot listings. Drivers should follow local rules, respect private property, and avoid unsafe behavior.

Open Photo Spots, compare approved locations, and check the rules when you arrive.
