Questions

Florida meet questions

What the city guides cover and where to check the current plan.

Can I use Facebook or Instagram to find Florida car meets?

Yes. Social posts can help you find a meet. Before you drive, check the original host or the meet page in Idle for the current time, location, and updates.

Can I find Florida car meets with Idle?

Yes. Open Idle to see Florida meets posted by hosts, including group meets you have access to.

Does this page list every Florida meet?

No. The city pages are search guides, not live event calendars.

Can hosts post Florida meets on Idle?

Yes. Hosts can add the time, location, flyer, parking notes, and ride-out details, then update the post if plans change.

Are private or group-only meets shown publicly?

No. Restricted meet details are only shown to people with access.
