Car meet discovery questions.

Clear answers for drivers searching for their next meet and hosts deciding where to post the plan.

Can I find local car meets with Idle?

Yes. Idle helps iPhone users discover local meets when they are posted in the app and available to them.

Can private or group-only meets show up for everyone?

No. Private, group-only, and invite-only details stay inside Idle for people with the right access.

Can hosts post car meets on Idle?

Yes. Hosts can post meet details, add parking or ride-out notes, share a clean flyer, and send updates from Idle.

Is Idle available on Android?

Idle is available for iPhone now. Android support is still in development and is not available yet.
