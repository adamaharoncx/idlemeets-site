Questions before you go

Finding a meet, checking the current plan, and understanding access.

How do I find car meets near me?

Search your city and nearby areas. Before you drive, check the original host or the meet page in Idle for the current time, location, parking notes, and updates.

Can I find local car meets with Idle?

Yes. Open Idle to see local meets posted by hosts, including group meets you have access to.

Can private or group-only meets show up for everyone?

No. Restricted meet details are only shown to people with access.

Can hosts post car meets on Idle?

Yes. Hosts can add the time, location, flyer, parking notes, and ride-out details, then update the post if plans change.

Is Idle available on Android?

Idle is available for iPhone now. Android support is still in development and is not available yet.
