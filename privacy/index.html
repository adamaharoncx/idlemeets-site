Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026. This page explains, in plain English, how Idle handles information for the website and the iPhone app.

Website privacy

This static website does not include accounts, forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or a database. If you open the site, GitHub Pages may receive basic technical information needed to deliver the page, such as your browser request and IP address.

App information

Idle is built around profiles, garages, meets, groups, messages, notifications, and safety features. When you use the app, Idle may process the information you choose to provide for those features.

  • Account and profile informationDetails such as your account, display profile, garage details, and settings.
  • Meet and community activityDetails connected to meets you create, join, view, or interact with.
  • Location-related informationLocation information may be used when you choose to discover nearby meets or share meet location details.
  • Messages and safety signalsMessage activity, moderation signals, blocking, reports, and notification data may be processed to keep the app functional and safer to use.

How information is used

Idle uses information to run the app, show relevant meet and garage experiences, support account features, send notifications when enabled, prevent abuse, fix bugs, and improve reliability.

Service providers

Idle may use trusted service providers for app infrastructure, database hosting, authentication, storage, notifications, and security. These providers help the app work; they are not there to sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can choose what information you add to your profile and garage, control app permissions through iOS Settings, and contact support with account or privacy questions.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact idle@engineer.com.

Short version

Idle should collect only what it needs to run the app, keep the experience useful, and support safety features.

Important

This policy should be reviewed regularly and updated if the app's data practices change.
