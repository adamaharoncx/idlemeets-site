Host flow

One page for the plan, the flyer, and the updates.

Idle gives organizers a structured place for the details people ask for over and over: when to show up, where to park, who is hosting, what the rules are, and what changed before the meet starts.

01

Post the plan

Create a meet people can actually follow.

  • Add the date, time, location, visibility, rules, and host context in one place.
  • Share public meets with the local scene, or keep group meets inside the right group.
  • Credit another host clearly when you are posting on their behalf.
02

Share cleanly

Turn the meet into a polished flyer.

  • Create and share a flyer directly from Idle so drivers can understand the plan quickly.
  • Add parking guidance, pre-meets, and ride-out details when attendees need extra context.
  • Keep sharing focused around one meet page instead of rebuilding the plan in every chat.
03

Host Tools

Manage the meet after it is live.

  • Review active, upcoming, and past hosted meets.
  • Send quick updates when timing, parking, weather, or details change.
  • Use Host Tools and Host again to bring back a successful meet with a fresh future date.

How to host

Give drivers the details they ask for anyway.

A good car meet page should answer the basics before people leave: where to go, when to arrive, who is hosting, where to park, and what changed before the meet starts.

Set the basics

Add the title, date, time, optional end time, location, visibility, rules, and host credit when you are posting on behalf of someone else.

Make the flyer useful

Use the meet details to create a flyer that carries the important information instead of sending people back through scattered captions and group chats.

Guide arrivals

Add parking guidance, pre-meets, and ride-out notes when the location or drive plan needs more explanation.

Update the plan

Use Host Tools for quick updates, detail changes, cancellation details, recaps, and hosting again when a meet comes back later.

Questions

Hosting questions.

Short answers for organizers deciding whether Idle fits the way they already host car meets.

Can I host a car meet on Idle?

Yes. Hosts can post meet details, choose visibility, share a flyer, add parking or ride-out notes, and send updates from Idle.

Can I use Idle for a private or group-only meet?

Idle supports public meets and group-based visibility, so hosts can keep group-only plans inside the right group.

Does Idle help with car meet flyers?

Idle helps hosts create and share a clean flyer from the meet details so drivers can understand the plan quickly.

Can hosts update a meet after posting?

Hosts can use Host Tools to manage supported meet details, send quick updates, adjust parking or ride-out notes, cancel when needed, and use Host again for a future date.

Want your meet on Idle?

Send the flyer, date, time, location, host name, and social link. You can also post it yourself in the app.
