Host flow

One page for the plan, the flyer, and the updates.

Idle gives organizers a structured place for the details people ask for over and over: when to show up, where to park, who is hosting, what the rules are, and what changed before the meet starts.

01

Post the plan

Create a meet people can actually follow.

  • Add the date, time, location, visibility, rules, and host context in one place.
  • Share public meets with the local scene, or keep group meets inside the right group.
  • Credit another host clearly when you are posting on their behalf.
02

Share cleanly

Turn the meet into a polished flyer.

  • Create and share a flyer directly from Idle.
  • Add parking guidance, pre-meets, and ride-out details when attendees need extra context.
  • Keep the share path focused so drivers can understand the plan quickly.
03

Host Tools

Manage the meet after it is live.

  • Review active, upcoming, and past hosted meets.
  • Send quick updates when timing, parking, weather, or details change.
  • Use Host again to bring back a successful meet with a fresh future date.

Want your meet on Idle?

Send the flyer, date, time, location, host name, and social link. You can also post it yourself in the app.
