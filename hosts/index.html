The meet page

The flyer can travel. The details stay put.

When someone opens the meet, they can see when to arrive, where to park, who is hosting, what the rules are, and whether the plan changed.

01

Post the plan

Publish once, then point everyone to the same meet.

  • Add the date, time, location, visibility, rules, and host credit.
  • Share a public meet broadly, or keep a group meet inside its group.
  • Credit another host clearly when you are posting on their behalf.
02

Make the flyer

Use the details you already entered.

  • Create and share a flyer directly from the meet instead of typing the basics again.
  • Add parking guidance, pre-meets, and ride-out details when attendees need extra context.
  • Link back to the meet page for details that do not fit on the flyer.
03

Host Tools

Weather changed? Update the meet.

  • Review active, upcoming, and past hosted meets.
  • Post an update when timing, parking, weather, or another detail changes.
  • Use Host Tools and Host again to bring back a successful meet with a fresh future date.

How to host

Start with the questions that show up in your DMs.

Where is it? What time? Who is hosting? Where do we park? Is it still on? Put those answers on the meet.

Set the basics

Add the title, date, time, optional end time, location, visibility, rules, and host credit when you are posting on behalf of someone else.

Make the flyer useful

Create the flyer from the meet details. Use the linked meet page for parking, ride-out, or change notes that need more room.

Guide arrivals

Add parking guidance, pre-meets, and ride-out notes when the location or drive plan needs more explanation.

Update the plan

Use Host Tools to change supported details, post updates, cancel, add a recap, or schedule the meet again for a new date.

Host checklist

Before you tap Post

A five-minute check can save a night of repeated questions and protect the venue that agreed to have you.

Confirm the venue

Make sure the location, operating hours, access, capacity, and any permission or venue requirements fit the meet you are planning before you share it publicly.

Set the full schedule

Publish the date, start time, optional end time, and any pre-meet or ride-out timing. Decide in advance how weather delays or cancellations will be communicated.

Plan the arrival

Explain the entrance, parking area, overflow plan, and any arrival order drivers need to follow. Add a parking plan when a single pin is not enough.

Choose visibility and rules

Decide whether the meet is public or group-only, credit the correct host, and make the venue, conduct, parking, and sharing expectations easy to understand.

Build a useful flyer

Show the meet name, date, start time, location or access instructions, host, and the rule or update people need most. Link the meet page for everything else.

Prepare the update path

Know where attendees will see a timing, weather, parking, or cancellation update. Use Host Tools so the latest plan stays attached to the meet.

Questions

What hosts usually ask

The basics about flyers, private meets, and changing a plan after it is posted.

What should a car meet flyer include?

Include the meet name, date, start time, location or access instructions, host, and the rule or update people need most. Keep parking, ride-out, and change details on the linked meet page.

Can I host a car meet on Idle?

Yes. Hosts can post meet details, choose visibility, share a flyer, add parking or ride-out notes, and send updates from Idle.

Can I use Idle for a group-only meet?

Idle supports public meets and group-based visibility, so hosts can keep group-only plans inside the right group.

Does Idle help with car meet flyers?

Yes. Idle creates a shareable flyer from the meet details you already entered.

Can hosts update a meet after posting?

Hosts can use Host Tools to manage supported meet details, send quick updates, adjust parking or ride-out notes, cancel when needed, and use Host again for a future date.

Have a meet that is already announced?

Send us the flyer and host details, or add the meet yourself in Idle.
