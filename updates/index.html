What's new in Idle.

Product updates, patch notes, and meaningful changes for Idle on iPhone.

Version 1.1.2 June 2026

Idle 1.1.2

Meet flyers are more polished, flexible, and personal, with stronger creative controls for hosts.

What's New

  • Step 2 of meet creation now has stronger flyer controls for text placement, layout, readability, color, opacity, font, and treatment options.
  • Logo customization is expanded with cleaner controls for choosing, sizing, and positioning the Idle mark or custom flyer branding.
  • Flyer editing from Host Tools now better matches the creation flow, so posted meet flyers can keep being refined after publishing.

Polish & Fixes

  • Flyer customizations save more reliably.
  • Custom flyer styling appears more consistently across meet cards and detail views.
Version 1.1.1 June 2026

Idle 1.1.1

Idle's biggest update yet brings CarPlay, Apple Wallet Idle Cards, host attribution, convoys, profile badges, Pulse controls, and active-meet tools into one release.

What's New

  • Upcoming mapped meets can appear in a clean CarPlay meet map with quick details and driving directions.
  • Apple Wallet Idle Cards let you keep your profile QR code ready for quick in-person sharing.
  • Posting for another host lets someone add a meet to Idle while crediting the original host.
  • Meets found on Instagram or Facebook can be brought into Idle with clearer attribution.
  • Active meet location update requests help hosts and attendees keep meet details accurate.
  • Hosts see location requests in Activity and can jump straight into Host Tools to send an update.
  • Convoys make it easier to coordinate the drive around a meet.
  • Founder, Kickstarter, and Sponsored badges add a cleaner identity layer to profiles.
  • Pulse now lets you choose between Nearby posts and All Idle.
  • Replies to Pulse comments are more streamlined.
  • Group-only meets stay limited to the right people, and invite-only meets stay private.

Polish & Fixes

  • Kept the CarPlay experience focused on upcoming meets, maps, and directions.
  • Added privacy-aware handling for restricted meet visibility on CarPlay.
  • Improved host credit language so shared meet details feel clear and respectful.
  • Profile badges have more polished styling across the app.
  • Home refreshes when your Pulse feed preference changes.
  • Improved profile and Home pager behavior.
  • Improved background notification support.
  • Added widget polish for a cleaner at-a-glance experience.
  • Improved routing around active meets, Host Tools, and meet notifications.
  • Added reliability fixes for active meet actions and notification behavior.
  • The tab bar now tucks away as you scroll down and returns when you scroll back up.

Version 1.0.5 May 2026

Idle 1.0.5

This update introduces Pulse, improves private group invites and profile links, and adds more polish across Home, messages, routing, and safety systems.

What's New

  • Pulse is here: a new Home tab for quick local car-scene updates, comments, reposts, and sharing public meets.
  • Private group invite links are here. Group admins can create a shareable link for private groups.
  • Invite links open cleanly in Idle, letting invited drivers preview and join the group.
  • Profile sharing is smoother with direct Idle profile links.
  • Profiles now support X links.
  • Archived meet links are clearer. Older meets still open, while RSVPs and planning actions stay closed.
  • Messages now support copying message text from the message menu.

Polish & Fixes

  • Improved deep-link routing after sign-in, onboarding, and legal agreement screens.
  • Nearby radius settings should stay put more reliably.
  • Smoothed out Home refresh and navigation behavior.
  • Refined sharing routes across meets, groups, and profiles.
  • Added more safety and reporting protections.

Version 1.0.3 May 2026

Idle 1.0.3

This update expands Garage to support bikes, improves vehicle identity across Idle, and gives hosts more control when editing flyers.

Added

  • Added support for bikes in Garage and onboarding.
  • You can now choose whether your vehicle is a car or bike when adding or editing your garage.
  • Added motorcycle brands and models, including Yamaha, Honda, Kawasaki, Ducati, Harley-Davidson, Suzuki, Triumph, KTM, BMW Motorrad, Aprilia, and Indian Motorcycle.
  • Expanded the car catalog with more classics and missing models, including older Volkswagen and Scion options.
  • Improved vehicle badges so cars and bikes display with the right icon and identity across profiles, meets, and garage surfaces.

Improved

  • Flyer editing now supports more control over text placement and appearance.
  • Host flyer tools now better preserve custom flyer styling.
  • Messages now handle cancelled background actions more quietly instead of showing unnecessary errors.
  • Sample data was updated to better reflect newer vehicle and meet behavior.
