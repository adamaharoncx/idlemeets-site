Version 1.2.1 July 2026

Idle 1.2.1

Better Groups. Richer Messages. Changed the oil, checked the links, and tightened the Group bolts.

What's New

  • Website links in Direct and Group messages can now display artwork, titles, and their source in a clean preview.
  • Meet and Convoy links continue using their purpose-built Idle cards, keeping important details easy to recognize.
  • Group owners have clearer tools for updating Group details, managing their community, and closing a Group when needed.
  • Idle prepares website previews privately, without your iPhone contacting the shared website just because you opened a conversation.

Polish & Fixes

  • Message reactions, reply layouts, loading states, and smaller chat interactions received additional polish.
  • Sharing and organizing feel more natural across Direct Messages and Groups.
  • Additional reliability improvements and smaller fixes make Idle feel more consistent throughout the app.

Version 1.1.9 July 2026

Idle 1.1.9

Your build. Your crew. One much bigger Idle. Version 1.1.9 brings one of our largest collections of improvements yet.

What's New

  • Build & Performance gives every Garage vehicle a deeper story, with modifications organized by category.
  • Record power and torque figures, add dyno-measured or owner-reported results, track performance times, and include optional photos to support your results.
  • Build details respect the vehicle owner's existing Garage privacy.
  • Private accounts and connection requests give you more control over who can see and interact with your content.
  • Private profiles remain discoverable, while Garage content, direct messaging, and connection-only updates stay protected until a request is accepted.
  • Meet sharing is now built throughout Idle. Send a Meet directly to a connection, share it with a Group, attach a public Meet to Pulse, or share it through your favorite apps.
  • Shared Meets appear as clean, useful cards inside Messages.
  • Group conversations now support photos, replies, hearts, mentions, typing and seen indicators, clearer previews, and more reliable messaging if your connection drops.
  • Start or reopen a Convoy from Group chat, then send a clear Convoy invite to connections or Groups.
  • Photos shared through Messages and Pulse now transition smoothly into a full-screen viewer and back to the original message or post.
  • Pinch or double-tap to zoom in on photos, and save them directly to your photo library.
  • A new indicator makes fresh Pulse posts easier to spot.
  • Ruby Star is now available as a new appearance theme.

Polish & Fixes

  • CarPlay Meet discovery is smoother, and signed-in drivers are no longer incorrectly asked to sign in again while their iPhone session is restoring.
  • CarPlay updates automatically as soon as your profile is ready.
  • Notifications do a better job protecting private details, and switching between accounts is more reliable.
  • Sidebar Attended and Connections stats now show a neutral loading state instead of an inaccurate zero, then update when the real count is available.
  • Writing and editing feels more reliable, and inbox swipe gestures are smoother.
  • Loading is smoother and more dependable across everyday Idle experiences.
