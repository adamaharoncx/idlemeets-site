Updates

What changed in Idle

Release notes for the iPhone app, newest version first.

Version 1.2.4 August 2026

Idle 1.2.4

Version 1.2.4 expands the Forums rollout, adds Google sign-in and connected sign-in methods, and brings more moderation, notification, and reliability improvements.

Forums Are Rolling Out

  • Idle Forums give the community a focused place to ask questions, share knowledge, discuss builds, and keep useful conversations easy to find.
  • Search threads, save favorites, mark helpful contributions, and revisit everything you have posted or replied to from My Posts.
  • New reply notifications take you directly back to the conversation.

One Account, More Ways In

  • Google sign-in is now available alongside Apple and email/password.
  • From Settings > Account > Sign-in methods, connect multiple sign-in options to the same Idle account.
  • Once linked, each method opens the same profile and keeps your Garage, Messages, Groups, Meets, and Connections together.
  • Idle protects you from accidentally removing your final sign-in method and provides clearer guidance for Apple Private Relay and email/password setup.

More Polish Throughout

  • Forum moderation and account notices are clearer and more dependable.
  • Message and Group notification cleanup is more precise.
  • Additional reliability improvements make everyday use smoother throughout Idle.
Version 1.2.3 August 2026

Idle 1.2.3

Version 1.2.3 opens Forums for invitation-only early access and makes addresses and phone numbers more useful in Messages.

Forums Early Access

  • A select group of drivers will begin receiving invitations while Idle tests real conversations and fine-tunes the experience before a wider release.
  • Early-access members can explore Forum discussions, search for conversations, save threads, and mark standout contributions as Helpful.
  • Everyone who has not received early access will continue seeing the Forums coming-soon screen for now.

Smarter Messages

  • Send a complete street address in a Direct or Group conversation, then tap it for directions through Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.
  • Phone numbers in conversations are now tappable, with a confirmation before Idle places the call.

Version 1.1.9 July 2026

Idle 1.1.9

Version 1.1.9 adds Build & Performance, private accounts, meet sharing, fuller Group messages, improved photo viewing, and Ruby Star.

What's New

  • Build & Performance gives every Garage vehicle a deeper story, with modifications organized by category.
  • Record power and torque figures, add dyno-measured or owner-reported results, track performance times, and include optional photos to support your results.
  • Build details respect the vehicle owner's existing Garage privacy.
  • Private accounts and connection requests give you more control over who can see and interact with your content.
  • Private profiles remain discoverable, while Garage content, direct messaging, and connection-only updates stay protected until a request is accepted.
  • Meet sharing is now built throughout Idle. Send a Meet directly to a connection, share it with a Group, attach a public Meet to Pulse, or share it through your favorite apps.
  • Shared Meets appear as native cards inside Messages with the plan easy to open.
  • Group conversations now support photos, replies, hearts, mentions, typing and seen indicators, clearer previews, and more reliable messaging if your connection drops.
  • Start or reopen a Convoy from Group chat, then send a clear Convoy invite to connections or Groups.
  • Photos shared through Messages and Pulse now transition smoothly into a full-screen viewer and back to the original message or post.
  • Pinch or double-tap to zoom in on photos, and save them directly to your photo library.
  • A new indicator makes fresh Pulse posts easier to spot.
  • Ruby Star is now available as a new appearance theme.

Fixes

  • CarPlay no longer incorrectly asks signed-in drivers to sign in again while their iPhone session is restoring.
  • CarPlay updates automatically as soon as your profile is ready.
  • Notifications do a better job protecting private details, and switching between accounts is more reliable.
  • Sidebar Attended and Connections stats now show a neutral loading state instead of an inaccurate zero, then update when the real count is available.
  • Writing and editing recover better from interruptions, and inbox swipe gestures are easier to complete.
  • More everyday screens keep their place and show accurate loading state while data arrives.
