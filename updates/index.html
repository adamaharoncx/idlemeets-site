Version 1.0.4 May 2026

Idle 1.0.4

This update adds quality-of-life improvements across meets, sharing, groups, and vehicle profiles.

What's New

  • Meet sharing is easier now. You can share a meet directly from the meet detail screen with a clean Idle link.
  • Hosts can see share activity. Host Tools now shows how many times a meet has been shared, giving hosts a better sense of interest before people pull up.
  • Flyer sharing feels smoother. Flyer sharing now uses a cleaner native share flow, making it easier to send flyers through Messages, Instagram, and other apps.
  • Group leaders can edit group details. Group owners can now update their group description and bio directly from the group page.
  • Vehicle catalog improvements. The vehicle catalog has been expanded with more Ferrari models.

Polish & Fixes

  • Improved share-link behavior across meet details and Host Tools.
  • Added better support for tracking shared meets.
  • Added backend support for meet share counts.
  • Added additional safety checks to keep older meet data working correctly.
Version 1.0.3 May 2026

Idle 1.0.3

This update expands Garage to support bikes, improves vehicle identity across Idle, and gives hosts more control when editing flyers.

Added

  • Added support for bikes in Garage and onboarding.
  • You can now choose whether your vehicle is a car or bike when adding or editing your garage.
  • Added motorcycle brands and models, including Yamaha, Honda, Kawasaki, Ducati, Harley-Davidson, Suzuki, Triumph, KTM, BMW Motorrad, Aprilia, and Indian Motorcycle.
  • Expanded the car catalog with more classics and missing models, including older Volkswagen and Scion options.
  • Improved vehicle badges so cars and bikes display with the right icon and identity across profiles, meets, and garage surfaces.

Improved

  • Flyer editing now supports more control over text placement and appearance.
  • Host flyer tools now better preserve custom flyer styling.
  • Messages now handle cancelled background actions more quietly instead of showing unnecessary errors.
  • Sample data was updated to better reflect newer vehicle and meet behavior.
