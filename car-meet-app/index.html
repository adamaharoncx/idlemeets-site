Why these features

It starts with the questions people actually ask.

  • Where are we parking? Who is hosting? What changed? What does that car have done to it?

Before you download another event app

Look past the map.

Finding a pin is easy. Knowing whether the meet is current, public, and still at that pin is the useful part.

A source you can verify

The meet should show its host and latest update, not just a screenshot copied from somewhere else.

The whole plan

The flyer, date, time, location, host, parking notes, rules, RSVP options, and updates should stay together instead of being split across screenshots and chats.

Tools after publishing

Hosts should be able to add arrival details, post an update, cancel when needed, and schedule the meet again later.

Visibility that means something

A public meet should be shareable. A group-only plan should stay with the group.

A reason to come back

Garage profiles, connections, groups, and messages make the people and cars from last night easier to find today.

Questions

A few straight answers

What Idle is, what hosts can do, and what happens to private meets.

Is Idle a car meetup app?

Yes. Idle is an iPhone app for finding and hosting in-person car meets. Meet details, host tools, flyers, garage profiles, groups, and messages are part of the same app.

What is Idle?

Idle is an iPhone app for finding and hosting car meets. It also includes garage profiles, car groups, messages, E85 tools, photo spots, and Convoys.

Can I host a car meet on Idle?

Yes. Hosts can post the time and place, add parking or ride-out notes, create a flyer, and update the meet after it is live.

Is Idle only for public car meets?

No. Hosts can post public meets or keep a meet inside a group. What a driver sees depends on the meet's visibility and their access.

Does Idle replace car meet flyers?

No. The flyer still helps people notice and share the meet. Idle keeps the current time, location, host, parking, rules, and updates behind it.
