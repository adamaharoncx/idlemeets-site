Questions

Car meet app questions.

Short answers for drivers, hosts, and groups deciding whether Idle fits the way they already meet up.

What is Idle?

Idle is an iPhone app for discovering nearby car meets, hosting meets, building your garage, messaging connections, and staying connected with local car groups.

Can I host a car meet on Idle?

Yes. Hosts can post meet details, add parking notes, share clean flyers, send updates, and keep attendees organized before the meet starts.

Is Idle only for public car meets?

Idle supports public meets and group-based meet visibility, so organizers can keep the right plans visible to the right drivers.

Does Idle replace car meet flyers?

Idle helps turn a flyer into a complete meet plan with date, time, location, host context, parking, rules, updates, and sharing.
