About Idle

Meet plans should not live in six different places.

Idle is an independent iPhone app for the details behind a car meet: the host, time, place, parking notes, updates, cars, groups, and messages.

Download on the App Store Explore features Product updates

Why Idle exists

The flyer is only part of the plan.

A flyer may be in one post, parking instructions in another, and a weather update buried in chat. Idle gives the host one meet page to keep current.

Before and after

Check the details. Then remember who showed up.

  • Drivers can check the time, place, host, parking plan, and latest update before leaving.
  • Hosts can change the plan without rebuilding it in every caption and group chat.
  • Garage profiles, connections, groups, and messages make it easier to find people again afterward.

Product choices

Car culture decides what belongs in Idle.

  • Meet pages include host credit, parking, pre-meets, ride-outs, and updates because those details matter on the day.
  • Garages, E85 tools, photo spots, groups, Convoys, and Pulse sit beside the meet instead of in unrelated apps.
  • Features earn their place by solving a problem drivers or organizers actually have.

Visibility

Some plans are not public.

  • The website explains the product without publishing private meet or group details.
  • Group-only and invite-only plans stay inside Idle for the people they were shared with.
  • Hosts choose the audience when they post the plan.

Behind Idle
