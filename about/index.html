About Idle

Built for car meets that happen in the real world.

Idle is an independently built iPhone app that gives drivers, hosts, and local groups one place for meet plans, garage profiles, messages, and the connections that continue after everyone leaves.

Download on the App Store Explore features Product updates

Why Idle exists

Make real car culture easier to follow.

Meet details often live across a flyer, a caption, several chats, and a last-minute update. Idle brings those pieces closer together while keeping hosts in control and private plans in the right places.

01

The whole meet

Useful before and after everyone arrives.

  • Before the meet, drivers can understand the time, place, host, parking plan, and latest updates.
  • Hosts can publish a complete plan, share a clean flyer, and keep attendees informed.
  • After the meet, garage profiles, connections, groups, and messages help real relationships continue.
02

Purpose-built

Designed around car culture, not a generic event template.

  • Meet discovery, Host Tools, garages, groups, Convoys, Pulse, and nearby car tools belong to one connected product.
  • The app is shaped around the questions drivers and organizers already ask every week.
  • Idle stays focused on making meets easier to find, easier to host, and easier to remember.
03

Thoughtful access

Public when useful. Private when it matters.

  • The public website explains Idle, its features, and how to use the app without exposing private meet details.
  • Group-only and invite-only plans stay inside Idle for people with the right access.
  • Clear visibility choices help organizers decide where a plan belongs.
04

Independent product

Built and operated by Adam Aharon.

  • Idle is an independently built product and service operated by Adam Aharon.
  • Public release notes show what is changing, while the Help Center documents how supported features work.
  • Questions and support requests can be sent to support@idlemeets.com.
