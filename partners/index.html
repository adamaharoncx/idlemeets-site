Understanding the gold check

The badge and the partnership.

The gold check is the default appearance of Idle’s Business or Host designation. A subscription colour can change its appearance without changing the designation.

A Community Partner listing reflects a separate agreement with Idle. A gold badge on its own does not mean a community is a partner.
