Idle on iPhone Duo: A First Look for Car-Meet Hosts

Idle’s iPhone Duo preview puts the flyer and its editing tools next to each other.

Preview October 13, 2026 By Idle

On this page

  1. A closer look at the workspace
  2. Keep the flyer connected to the meet
  3. Prepare the format people will see
  4. Check the available version

In the demo shown here, Flyer Studio uses the unfolded landscape layout for a large preview on the left and an editing panel on the right. This keeps the overall design visible while hosts work on its details.

This screenshot uses sample content from a preview build of Idle. The downloadable version may differ.

A closer look at the workspace

The editing panel shows Size, Photo, Text and Look. The Layers controls in Text let hosts choose an individual detail to work on while the flyer stays visible beside the tools.

Use the larger preview to review the title, image and supporting details together. Check that the date, time and location remain readable against the photo as you adjust the design.

Keep the flyer connected to the meet

The flyer communicates the essentials: the meet name, date, time and location. The linked meet details give attendees room for parking guidance, arrival instructions and changes to the plan.

Idle creates the flyer from details entered for the meet. Share the meet page alongside it so people can check the current plan. The hosting overview explains how flyers, arrival notes and Host Tools work together.

Prepare the format people will see

Feed, Square and Story have different proportions. Inspect the photo crop and text in each format you intend to export, then check the exported image before sharing.

The full flyer guide covers photos, logos, text layers, saving changes and exports.

Check the available version

The Duo layout shown here is a preview. Check Idle’s release notes and the Idle App Store listing for the available version. This preview does not confirm that the shown Duo layout is available in the current download.
