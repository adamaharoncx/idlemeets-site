Account

I did not receive a password reset email

Use these checks if you requested a reset link but do not see the email.

Check these first

  • Use the exact account emailEnter the same email address you used when creating your Idle account.
  • Check spam, junk, and promotionsSearch your inbox for Idle or password reset.
  • Wait a few minutesEmail delivery is usually fast, but it can be delayed.
  • Request one new linkIf the first email does not arrive, try the reset flow once more after a few minutes.
  • Open the newest emailOlder reset links may stop working after a newer one is requested.

If you use Sign in with Apple

Idle does not send password reset emails for your Apple ID. Use Apple account recovery or your iPhone Settings to manage your Apple ID password.

Contact support

If the reset email still does not arrive, email support@idlemeets.com. Include the email address you are trying to use, your iPhone model if you know it, and roughly when you requested the reset.
