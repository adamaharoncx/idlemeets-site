Pulse

How Pulse works

Pulse is Idle's quick car-scene feed for short updates, photos, comments, likes, reposts, mentions, polls, and public meet cards.

Common actions

  • PostShare a short update or photo from the local scene.
  • Comment and replyOpen a post to join the conversation.
  • Like or repostReact to posts and bring useful updates back into the feed.
  • MentionMention drivers or groups when they are relevant and visible to you.
  • Attach a meetShare public meet cards so people can open the full plan.

What to expect

Some content may not appear if it was deleted, reported, blocked, hidden, or outside what your account is allowed to see. Group-only, invite-only, private, and connections-only meets should not be shared into public Pulse.

Troubleshooting

  • Post failedCheck the message, edit if needed, and retry.
  • Photo failedTry a smaller supported image and check your connection.
  • Notification opens ActivityThe original Pulse content may no longer be available.

Report Pulse content

If a Pulse post or comment is abusive, spammy, misleading, or unsafe, use the in-app report option or email support@idlemeets.com.
