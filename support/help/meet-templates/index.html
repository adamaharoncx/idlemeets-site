Hosting

How meet templates work

Meet templates are private reusable setups. Save the details you use often, bring them into a new meet, then review everything before publishing.

These screenshots use sample content in a preview version of Idle. Your app may look different. Select an image to see the full screen.

Save a setup you want to reuse

  1. Prepare the meet details and flyer.
  2. Continue to the Share stage and choose Save as template. Host Tools also has a Save template entry for a hosted setup.
  3. Give the template a clear name and confirm the save.

After saving a template, the Share stage shows its name, Update template and Save as new template. Saving a template does not publish a meet.

Find your saved template

Open Host. Saved setups appear under Templates, separately from Unfinished drafts.

Choose a template to start another meet from that setup. Templates created during a test drive stay on that iPhone. For templates saved to an account, check that you are using the same Idle account.

Review everything before publishing

  1. Select the template you want to use.
  2. Review the suggested date and local time, including any optional end time.
  3. Check the title, location, visibility, flyer, parking plan, ride-out plan, and other reused details.
  4. Publish only when the new meet is ready.

Always check the start and end dates when you reuse a template.

Manage saved templates

Open the template’s More actions menu for Rename, Duplicate, or Delete.

Use Update template in a setup linked to an existing template when you want to revise that saved setup, or Save as new template to keep a separate one.

Templates are not recurring meets

A template is a reusable starting point. It does not publish on a schedule, copy attendance, or create a live meet until you review the setup and publish it.

Rename and duplicate a saved setup

Open More actions → Rename to change the template name, or Duplicate to make a separate copy. Confirm the name you want to use.

Templates and unfinished drafts are listed separately. Choose the item you want to work on.

Save changes to a template

Open the template you want to change and edit the meet details. Continue through Flyer → Continue to share, then choose Update template to save those details for future use.

Check the template name in the confirmation before choosing Update. Choose Cancel if it is not the setup you intended to replace.

Prepare your next meet

Choose a saved setup under Host → Templates. Edit the title for your next meet and set its start and end time. Review the location, audience and flyer before publishing.

The template name identifies the saved setup. The meet title is what attendees see; change it when your next meet needs a different name.

Troubleshooting

  • Template is missingConfirm you are signed into the same Idle account that saved it.
  • A group option changedGroup access is checked again when you reuse a template. Choose an eligible group or another visibility option.
  • Date or time looks wrongReview the suggested future date and local time before publishing.
  • Template will not loadTry again, or start a blank meet. A template problem should not stop you from hosting.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the template name, what you tapped, and the exact message shown.
