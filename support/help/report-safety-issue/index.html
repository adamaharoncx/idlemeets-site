Safety

Report a safety issue

Tell Idle about activity that feels unsafe, abusive, spammy, misleading, or harmful.

What to report

  • Unsafe meet behaviorDangerous driving, illegal activity, or host instructions that could put people at risk.
  • Abuse or harassmentThreats, targeted harassment, hate, or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Spam or misleading contentFake meets, scam links, impersonation, or misleading group activity.
  • Privacy concernsPrivate information shared without permission.

What to include

Email idle@engineer.com with the meet, group, profile, message, or username involved. Include screenshots if they help explain the issue.

Emergency situations

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. Idle support is not an emergency response service.
