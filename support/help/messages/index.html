Messages

How messages work

Direct messages are for accepted connections. They help you coordinate before and after meets, share photos, and keep the conversation moving when a send needs to be retried.

Common actions

  • Message connectionsOpen a conversation from Messages or a connected profile.
  • Use statusesShare a short status with connections for about 24 hours.
  • Send Convoy invitesWhen you are in an active Convoy, you can send an invite card to an accepted connection.
  • React and retryHeart messages. If a send fails, tap Retry to try the same message again.
  • Open shared photosTap a photo to open it full screen. Pinch or double-tap to zoom, use the download button to save it, and close or swipe vertically to return when it is not zoomed.
  • Use safety controlsReport messages or block accounts when needed.

What to expect

  • Connections onlyMessages are for accepted connections. Privacy, blocking, account state, or connection changes can affect whether a thread is visible or usable.
  • StatusesStatuses are short notes for connections and can expire after about 24 hours.
  • Convoy invitesConvoy invite cards help a connection join an active Convoy, but they should not show live location before the recipient joins.
  • Temporary connection lossOpen conversations refresh recent messages after the connection returns.
  • Saving photosSaving a shared photo requires permission to add it to your iPhone Photos library.
  • Reduce MotionFull-screen photo transitions follow your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting.
  • LinksExternal links in messages may ask for confirmation before leaving Idle.

Troubleshooting

  • Message delayed or failedCheck your connection, reopen the thread, and tap Retry if the option appears. A retry continues the same message rather than creating another one.
  • Photo cannot be retriedIf the original photo is no longer available, choose it again and send a new message.
  • Photo will not saveCheck Idle's Photos permission in iPhone Settings, then try again.
  • Convoy invite will not sendConfirm the Convoy is still active and the recipient is an accepted connection.
  • Invite card will not joinThe Convoy may have ended, expired, or become full.
  • Status disappearedShort statuses are temporary and can expire.
  • Thread missingBlocking, account state, or access changes can affect message visibility.

Contact support

Do not send private message content unless needed for a report. For help, email support@idlemeets.com with the usernames involved, approximate time, and screenshot if useful.
