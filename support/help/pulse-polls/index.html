Pulse

How Pulse polls work

Pulse polls are quick questions for meet plans, routes, opinions, and local car-scene decisions.

These screenshots use sample content in a preview version of Idle. Your app may look different. Select an image to see the full screen.

Read an open poll

Open Home → Pulse to find polls in the feed. A poll can show its question, answer choices, vote count, and time remaining.

Read the question and choose carefully. Pulse polls are designed for one vote per account while the poll is open.

Create a question and choices

  1. Open Pulse from Home.
  2. Tap the pencil compose button for New Pulse post.
  3. Choose the poll icon to show Ask a question and two initial choice fields.
  4. Enter a clear question and distinct answer choices.

Current Pulse polls cannot be combined with photo posts or public meet cards.

Add or remove an extra choice

Tap Add choice when the question needs another answer. An added choice has an × control for removing it.

Keep the options easy to tell apart. Use Remove poll if you want to return to a text post instead.

Review the poll length

Use Days, Hours and Minutes to set the poll length. Days offers 0 through 7, and Minutes offers five-minute steps. Check the full duration before posting.

Post the completed poll

Review the question, choices and Poll length, then choose Post poll. The example below is set to five minutes.

If Post poll is unavailable, check that you have entered the question, choices and poll length.

Vote and read the results

Tap a choice to vote. The checkmark shows your selected choice, and the percentages show the results so far.

Check the final results

Look for Final results after the poll closes.

If the time or results look out of date, switch to Meets and return to Pulse to check again.

Troubleshooting

  • Poll will not postCheck that the question and choices are clear and complete.
  • Cannot vote againPulse polls are designed for one vote per account.
  • Poll closedClosed polls can show final results instead of accepting new votes.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com if a poll behaves unexpectedly or shows content that should be reviewed.
