Account

Manage your sign-in methods

Sign in with Apple, Google, or email, and manage linked methods from one Idle account.

Sign in with Apple

  1. Open Idle while signed out.
  2. Tap Continue with Apple.
  3. Complete the Apple sign-in prompt.
  4. A returning account opens its existing Idle profile. A new account continues through normal profile setup.

Sign in with Google

  1. Open Idle while signed out.
  2. Tap Continue with Google.
  3. Complete Google sign-in in the browser window.
  4. A linked returning account opens its existing Idle profile. A new account continues through normal profile setup.

Already have an Idle account?

Add Google from Settings > Account > Sign-in methods before signing out. This keeps your profile, garage, connections, groups, messages, memberships, and settings together. Do not create a second account to work around a linking problem.

Review or add a sign-in method

  1. Open Idle while signed in.
  2. Go to Settings > Account > Sign-in methods.
  3. Review Apple, Google, and Email and password.
  4. Tap Add beside an eligible method and complete the steps shown.
  5. Return to Sign-in methods and confirm the method says Linked.

Add or reset a password

  • Add passwordIf you created your account with Apple or Google and see Add password, follow the steps to add email/password access to that same Idle account.
  • Reset passwordIf your account already uses email and password, choose Send password reset link and confirm before Idle sends the email.
  • Keep your current passwordYour current password stays active unless you open the reset link and finish choosing a new one.

Remove a linked method

You can remove Apple or Google only when another usable sign-in method remains. Idle will not let you remove your final way back into the account.

Troubleshooting

  • Refresh neededRefresh Sign-in methods before adding or removing a method.
  • UnavailableKeep using a method that is already linked and try again later.
  • Apple Private RelayYour Apple sign-in email may look different from your usual email. Keep using the Apple method already connected to your Idle account.
  • Another account appearsStop and return to your original sign-in method. Do not continue by creating another Idle account.
  • Sign-in canceledReopen Sign-in methods and start again when you are ready.
  • Cannot access the accountUse the original sign-in method or email support before changing anything else.

Privacy

Sign-in details are account settings, not public profile information. Idle does not use an Apple or Google email address to combine separate accounts.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com if a method will not link or the wrong account appears. Include the sign-in method you were using, what appeared on screen, and a screenshot if useful. Never send your password.
