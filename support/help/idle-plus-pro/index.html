Memberships

Customize your presence on Idle.

Idle Plus and Idle Pro are optional memberships for profile cosmetics. Core meets, groups, messages, profiles, hosting, garages, and connections stay free.

Cosmetic profile style Optional support for Idle Core features stay free

Core Idle stays free.

You do not need Idle Plus or Idle Pro to use the main parts of Idle.

  • Browse meets
  • Host meets
  • Join groups
  • Message eligible connections
  • Use profiles and garages
  • Use safety tools
  • Receive available notifications
  • Access your account

Choose the cosmetic level that fits you.

Both memberships are optional. They change profile style, not core access.

Idle Plus

$4.99/month

Support Idle and add static profile cosmetics.

  • Purple supporter verification badge
  • Static profile photo ring
  • Static profile card decor
  • Ring and decor color choices
  • More Plus cosmetics over time

Idle Pro

$6.99/month

Includes Idle Plus and adds more expressive profile identity.

  • Everything in Idle Plus
  • Black supporter verification badge
  • Animated profile photo ring
  • Animated profile card decor
  • Future Pro cosmetic options

Find your profile style settings

Open Profile → Edit → Profile style to find Verification badge, Avatar ring and Banner accent. Available options depend on your account and membership.

Choose an avatar ring

Open Avatar ring, then choose a ring style and colour. Choose None if you do not want a ring.

Available styles depend on your account and membership. Check the options offered for your account.

Choose a banner accent

Open Banner accent to choose None, Static accent or Animated accent.

Supporter colours and cosmetic styling stay separate from Staff and Business or Host meanings. Silver is only for official Idle Staff accounts. See What profile badges mean.

What membership does not mean

Not a safety signal

A membership does not verify driving behavior, meet safety, hosting quality, or off-app identity.

Not official approval

A membership does not make a profile official Idle Staff, a verified business, or a verified host.

No special priority

A membership does not prioritize a profile in discovery, support, reporting, moderation, or meet hosting.

Troubleshooting

If a style option looks locked or does not update, confirm your membership is active, restore purchases in Idle if that option is shown, then refresh the profile.

Billing help

Subscription billing is managed by Apple through your App Store account. Use your iPhone subscription settings to cancel or change plans.

Need help?

Email support@idlemeets.com with your Idle handle, the issue you are seeing, your device, and a screenshot if it helps. Do not send passwords or payment details. Include only the account information needed to explain the issue.
