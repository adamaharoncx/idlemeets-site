Private groups

How private group requests work

Private groups may ask for extra context before approving access. This helps group admins understand who is requesting to join.

Screenshots show sample content from the Idle test drive in a prerelease build, version 1.3.0 (1), using Dark appearance and Alpine White. Your app may look different. Sample requests, messages, status labels, and counts do not show live delivery or access changes. Learn about the test drive.

Request access when available

  1. Open the private group.
  2. Tap the request access option if it is available.
  3. Select the vehicle or context the group asks for.
  4. Add any required photo or short note.
  5. Send the request and wait for a group owner or admin to review it.

Check a pending request

Open Groups from the Home sidebar, then choose Requests. Scroll to Requests pending to review a request’s current status.

A pending request is still waiting for a group owner or admin to review it.

Review an invitation

In Requests, Invites for you shows the group and who invited you. Read the invitation before choosing Accept invite or Decline.

For group owners and admins

When you have the appropriate group role, Requests to review can show the requester, vehicle, and short verification note. Review that context alongside your group’s rules before choosing Accept or Decline.

Check what changed after acceptance

Accepting the seeded Harbor Lot Society invitation opened the group with Joined. After the sample Cityline Drivers review was accepted, its review card disappeared and Yours showed four members.

The separate Tri-State Supras request still showed Pending and Waiting on admins. That request was already present; it was not newly submitted.

All changes stayed in the local test drive. Fresh request submission, decline, live approval, and access enforcement were not tested.

What to expect

Private group content and group-only meets should be available only after access is accepted. Admins may approve or decline requests based on the group's rules.

Troubleshooting

  • Request cannot sendCheck that required vehicle, photo, or note fields are complete.
  • No response yetGroup admins may need time to review.
  • Access still missing after approvalRefresh groups or reopen Idle.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the group name, your username, and request status if something seems stuck.
