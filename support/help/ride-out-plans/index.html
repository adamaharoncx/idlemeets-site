Ride-outs

How ride-out plans work

A ride-out plan tells attendees where the group plans to go after the meet and when the drive should leave.

For hosts

  1. Create or edit a meet.
  2. Add a ride-out destination, leave time, and note when the meet includes a planned drive.
  3. Review the details before publishing or saving from Host Tools.
  4. Send a quick host update if the plan changes before the meet.

What to expect

Ride-out plans are planning notes for attendees. They do not replace safe driving, local laws, or directions from the maps app.

Troubleshooting

  • Directions are wrongCollect the destination name, address or city, and which maps app opened.
  • Leave time looks wrongAsk the host to check the meet time and ride-out time in Host Tools.
  • Plan missingThe host may not have added a ride-out plan.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the meet title, ride-out destination, and a screenshot if possible.
