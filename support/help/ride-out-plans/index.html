Ride-outs

How ride-out plans work

A ride-out plan tells attendees where the group plans to go after the meet and when the drive should leave.

These screenshots use sample content in a preview version of Idle. Your app may look different. Select an image to see the full screen.

Add the next stop

  1. Create a meet or open a draft in the Host flow.
  2. Find Ride-out plan and choose Add. The fields open in the Create form.
  3. Enter the destination in Next spot and its Address.
  4. Review the Leave time for the planned drive.

The ride-out section is optional. Its Close control collapses the fields in the draft form.

Add a useful quick note

Use Quick note for extra context about the plan. Review the destination, address, leave time, and note before publishing or saving from Host Tools.

If the plan changes before the meet, send a quick host update with the corrected details.

Save your ride-out plan

For an existing meet, open Host Tools → Upcoming → Open tools → Ride-out plan. Enter the next stop, address, leave time and note, then choose Save.

Find the plan on the meet page

Open the meet page and scroll to Ride-out plan. Check the next stop, address, leave time and note together.

Use the plan for the meet you are attending. The pictured destination is an example, so do not use it as directions for a real venue.

What to expect

Ride-out plans are planning notes for attendees. They do not replace safe driving, local laws, or directions from the maps app.

Troubleshooting

  • Directions are wrongCollect the destination name, address or city, and which maps app opened.
  • Leave time looks wrongAsk the host to check the meet time and ride-out time in Host Tools.
  • Plan missingThe host may not have added a ride-out plan.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the meet title, ride-out destination, and a screenshot if possible.
