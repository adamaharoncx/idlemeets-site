Widgets

How widgets and Live Activities work

Idle can show meet or Convoy context on supported iPhone system surfaces, such as widgets and Live Activities.

What they can show

  • Next Meet widgetShows your next public meet at a glance when Idle has current app data.
  • Meet-day Live ActivityCan appear near meet time for eligible meets you marked Going.
  • Convoy Live ActivityCan show active Convoy status while you are an active member.

What to expect

Widgets and Live Activities depend on iPhone support, account state, app state, eligible meets, and system timing. They are not a guarantee of exact background refresh timing.

Troubleshooting

  • Widget staleOpen Idle, refresh the app, then check the widget again.
  • Live Activity missingConfirm the meet is eligible and you marked Going near meet time.
  • Convoy Live Activity still showingOpen Idle, leave or end the Convoy if needed, and check again.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, the meet or Convoy, and a screenshot.
