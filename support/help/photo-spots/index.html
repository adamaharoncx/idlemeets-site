Photo spots

How photo spots work

Photo spots help drivers find community-rated places to shoot cars, scout clean backdrops, and recommend new locations for review.

Find photo spots

  1. Open Search in Idle.
  2. Open the photo spots area.
  3. Browse approved spots near your discovery area.
  4. Review the name, tags, rating, distance, and photos when available.
  5. Open directions if you decide to go.

Recommend or rate a spot

  • Recommend a spotAdd a name, location or city, helpful tags, and an optional photo.
  • Wait for reviewNew submissions may need review before they appear broadly.
  • Rate approved spotsAdd a rating or rating photo when the spot is available to you.
  • Manage your own contentYou may be able to update or remove your own submitted spot where the app allows it.

Troubleshooting

  • No spots nearbyTry a different discovery area or check again later.
  • Submission is not visibleIt may still be pending review or may not have been approved.
  • Location will not fill inCheck location permission or enter the location manually if available.
  • Photo failedTry a clearer supported image and check your connection.

Be respectful

Follow local rules, respect private property, and avoid unsafe behavior when visiting or recommending a photo spot.

Still need help?

Email support@idlemeets.com with the spot name, city, and a screenshot if the issue is visible.
