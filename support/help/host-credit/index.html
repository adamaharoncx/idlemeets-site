Host credit

Posting for another host and Host credit

Host credit lets a meet show the right public attribution when someone is posting on behalf of another host.

These screenshots use sample content in a preview version of Idle. Your app may look different. Select an image to see the full screen.

Choose who gets the credit

  1. In the Host flow, scroll to Posting for another host and turn it on when you are posting on someone else’s behalf.
  2. Choose Idle, Instagram, or Facebook.
  3. For Idle credit, use the Idle user field. Review the public host credit before publishing.

Host credit is public attribution. The signed-in poster still manages the Idle post, including edit, cancel, and Host Tools access.

Credit an Instagram or Facebook host

Choosing a social source changes the fields shown in the form:

  • InstagramEnter the host’s Display name and Instagram link.
  • FacebookEnter the host’s Display name and Facebook link.

Check that the name and link identify the right host before publishing. Adding credit does not transfer management of the Idle post.

If you are credited

If you are credited through an Idle profile, you may be able to confirm the credit or mark it Not mine. Check the credited meet for the options available to you.

Troubleshooting

  • Wrong host creditedIf the option appears, the credited person can choose Not mine.
  • Cannot confirm creditConfirm you are signed into the exact Idle account that was credited.
  • Social credit will not saveCheck that the Instagram or Facebook link is valid.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the meet title, credited account or link, and what needs to be corrected.
