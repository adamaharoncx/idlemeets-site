Location

How Convoy location sharing works

Convoy location sharing is optional and tied to an active Convoy. It is not meant to be broad public tracking.

Sharing options

  • Foreground sharingShare location while using Idle during the active Convoy.
  • Share While LockedContinue sharing while locked only when you choose that mode and iPhone Settings allows Idle Always Location.
  • Stop sharingTurn sharing off, leave the Convoy, or end the Convoy if you are the host.

What to expect

Other active members should only see shared location from members who choose to share. Sharing should stop when you stop sharing, leave, the host ends the Convoy, or the Convoy expires.

Troubleshooting

  • Location not visibleConfirm Location Services are on and that you tapped the share-location control.
  • Locked sharing stopsConfirm Idle has Always Location permission and that you chose Share While Locked.
  • Privacy concernStop sharing or leave the Convoy, then contact support if location still appears active.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the Convoy code, approximate time, permission state, and what you saw.
