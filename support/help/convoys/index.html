Convoys

How Convoys work

Convoys are short-lived spaces for active drive coordination. You can start or join with a code, link, or Messages invite, then choose whether to share location while you are in the Convoy.

Steps

  1. Open Convoys in Idle.
  2. Start a new Convoy or enter a join code from another driver.
  3. Share the code, link, or a Messages invite with the drivers who should join.
  4. Choose whether to share location while actively in the Convoy.
  5. Leave when done, or end the Convoy if you are the host.

What to expect

Convoys are meant for active members, not public discovery or permanent tracking. Expired, ended, or left Convoys should not keep acting like active drives.

Troubleshooting

  • Code will not joinConfirm the code is current and that you are signed in.
  • Messages invite will not joinThe Convoy may have ended, expired, or become full.
  • Link opens the websiteConfirm Idle is installed on that iPhone and try opening the link again.
  • Convoy looks inactiveIt may have ended or expired.

Privacy note

You should not see member locations until you join an active Convoy and members choose to share. For concerns, email support@idlemeets.com.

Driving safety

Set up or change Convoy settings while parked. Do not interact with Idle while driving.
