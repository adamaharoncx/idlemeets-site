Use Flyer Studio on iPhone Duo

Duo’s unfolded landscape layout gives your flyer more room. Keep the preview visible on the left while adjusting its design with the tools on the right.

This screenshot uses sample content from a preview build of Idle. The downloadable version may differ. Select the image to see the full-size screenshot.

  1. Open Flyer Studio while creating a meet, or open the flyer tools for a meet you host.
  2. Unfold Duo and use landscape orientation for the side-by-side layout.
  3. Use Size, Photo, Text and Look to adjust your flyer. In Text, choose a layer to work on individual details.
  4. Check the title, date, time and location in the large preview before continuing to share.

For the full guide to photos, logos and exports, read Edit and share meet flyers.
