Profile

What profile badges mean

Badges and verified checks add public context to a profile. They can appear next to a profile's name or handle in Idle where supported.

Verified checks

Blue Verified

This account is notable, identity-confirmed, or otherwise verified by Idle.

Silver Staff

This account is an official Idle Staff account. Silver is only for Idle Staff.

Gold Business or Host

This account represents a trusted business, organizer, venue, or host.

Idle Plus

This account is showing an Idle Plus subscription verified badge.

Idle Pro

This account is showing an Idle Pro subscription verified badge.

Profile badges

Founder

One of Idle's first 100 users. This is the rarest profile badge.

Kickstarter

An Idle promoter who helped support and grow the community early.

Sponsored

A sponsored profile, business, host, or partner recognized by Idle.

No badge

None

This profile does not currently have a public badge or verified check.

What to expect

  • Public contextBadges are meant to help people understand profile context at a glance.
  • Silver is staff-onlySilver Staff is reserved for official Idle Staff accounts and is not a membership color.
  • Subscription badges are separateIdle Plus and Idle Pro badges do not replace Staff or Business or Host checks.
  • Not every profile has oneMany normal Idle profiles do not have a badge or verified check.
  • Use normal judgmentA badge is not a guarantee of meet safety, driving behavior, or off-app identity.

If a badge looks wrong

Email support@idlemeets.com with the profile handle, what looks wrong, and a screenshot if it helps. Do not send private information unless support needs it for review.
