Sign in

Trouble accessing your account

Start with these checks if Idle is not letting you sign in.

Check your sign-in method

Use the same method you used when creating the account. If you used Sign in with Apple, keep using Sign in with Apple. If you used email and password, use the email option.

Common fixes

  • Update IdleInstall the latest version from the App Store.
  • Check your connectionTry Wi-Fi or cellular if one network is failing.
  • Restart the appClose Idle fully, reopen it, and try again.
  • Reset your passwordIf you use email sign-in, reset your password from the sign-in screen.

When to contact support

Email idle@engineer.com if you still cannot access your account. Include your email, the sign-in method you used, your iPhone model, and what error you see.
