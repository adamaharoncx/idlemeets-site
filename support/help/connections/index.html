Connections

How connections work

Connections are the drivers you choose to connect with in Idle. They can affect messaging, statuses, profile context, and who you see around meets.

These screenshots use sample content in a preview version of Idle. Your app may look different. Select an image to see the full screen.

Open your connections

Open Profile, then tap your connections count to open Connections. The list can show received and sent requests alongside accepted connections.

Received requests show Decline and Accept. A sent request can show Requested and Cancel.

Connect from a profile

Open a profile from Search, a meet, Activity, Messages, or a group. Use the connection button when it is available.

For an accepted connection, a profile can show Connected and a Message button. Tap Message when messaging is available to open the conversation.

What to expect

Some users may limit what connection details are visible. Counts and lists can differ by viewer because privacy settings and blocking can affect what is shown.

Troubleshooting

  • Connection button missingThe profile may not allow the action, or the account may be blocked or unavailable.
  • Count looks differentCollect screenshots of both places where the count appears.
  • Messages unavailableMessaging is for accepted connections.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the usernames involved and screenshots of the places where the connection count or status differs.
