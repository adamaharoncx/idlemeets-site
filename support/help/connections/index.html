Connections

How connections work

Connections are the drivers you choose to connect with in Idle. They can affect messaging, statuses, profile context, and who you see around meets.

Common actions

  1. Open a profile from Search, a meet, Activity, Messages, or a group.
  2. Use the connection button when it is available.
  3. Open your connections list from Profile or related surfaces.
  4. Message accepted connections when messaging is available.

What to expect

Some users may limit what connection details are visible. Counts and lists can differ by viewer because privacy settings and blocking can affect what is shown.

Troubleshooting

  • Connection button missingThe profile may not allow the action, or the account may be blocked or unavailable.
  • Count looks differentCollect screenshots of both places where the count appears.
  • Messages unavailableMessaging is for accepted connections.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the usernames involved and screenshots of the mismatched surfaces.
