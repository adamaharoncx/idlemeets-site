Why am I not seeing meets?

Idle uses your location and the latest feed refresh to show nearby meets. Start with these checks if Home looks empty.

Turn on location access

If location is off, Idle may not be able to surface nearby meets on Home or in discovery.

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Idle.
  3. Tap Location.
  4. Choose While Using the App.
  5. Turn on Precise Location if it is available.
  6. Reopen Idle.

Location should stay enabled

After you choose While Using the App, iPhone should keep that permission enabled. You usually only need to change it again if you turn it off later, reinstall Idle, or iOS asks you to confirm permissions again.

Refresh the Home feed

After changing location settings, refresh Home so Idle can check again.

  1. Open Idle.
  2. Go to Home.
  3. Pull down from the top of the feed until the refresh indicator appears.
  4. Release and wait for Home to finish refreshing.

Other reasons Home may look empty

  • No active nearby meetsThere may not be a public meet near you right now.
  • Private or group meetsSome meets only show to people in the right group.
  • Connection issuesTry switching between Wi-Fi and cellular, then refresh Home again.
  • Outdated app versionInstall the latest Idle update from the App Store.

Still not seeing meets?

Email support@idlemeets.com with your city, whether location is enabled, your iPhone model, iOS version, and a screenshot of Home if possible.
