Profile

Manage your garage and profile

Your profile holds your identity and privacy choices. Each garage vehicle can hold photos, Build & Performance entries, a primary-vehicle setting, and private fuel details for your own tools.

These screenshots use sample content in a preview version of Idle. Your app may look different. Select an image to see the full screen.

Edit your profile details

Open Profile from the bottom navigation, then choose Edit. Review your Name, Username, Home base, and Bio.

Scroll for Profile style and your social links. Use Save when your edits are ready, or Cancel to leave without applying them.

Profile style lets you customize your badge colour, Avatar ring and Banner accent where available. Some choices require Idle Plus or Idle Pro. The app's badge control is called Verification badge; changing its colour does not verify your identity. See Profile badges and Idle Plus and Pro.

Open your garage

On your profile, choose Garage, then Open to enter My Garage. Tap a vehicle for its details.

The primary vehicle is marked Primary and can appear around Idle next to your name. Swipe a vehicle left to reveal Edit and Delete; review carefully before changing or removing a vehicle.

Add a vehicle

Use the + control in My Garage to open the vehicle form. Review Car or Bike, then add the year, make, model, and trim where available.

The form also has Make primary and Add photos entries. Frame each chosen photo, position plate blur if you use it, then tap Use photo. Tap Add to create the vehicle.

Update your vehicle photos

  1. Open Profile > Garage > Open, swipe your vehicle left and tap Edit.
  2. Under Garage photos, use Add photos or Add more. Frame each photo, drag to reposition or pinch to zoom, then tap Use photo.
  3. Drag thumbnails to change their order; the first is the Lead photo. Tap a thumbnail's x to remove that photo.
  4. Tap Save to apply your vehicle edits.

Record your build and performance

Open a vehicle, scroll to Build & Performance, and choose Manage.

  • Add ModificationRecord the part, category, brand, and useful installation details. A supporting-photo entry is available.
  • Add Power FigureRecord how the figure was determined and whether it is wheel or crank output.
  • Add Performance ResultRecord the measurement and run details, with optional photo proof where supported.

Complete the record details, then tap Save. Open an existing record from the list to edit it.

Build & Performance is a record added by the vehicle owner. Idle has not confirmed those figures, and an attached photo does not guarantee their accuracy.

Keep power figures in context

The Add Power Figure form separates the reporting method, Wheel or Crank, horsepower and torque, and setup notes.

Enter the figures you want to record and explain how they were measured. Include useful setup details so other people can understand them.

Find the vehicle’s fuel tools

In vehicle details, scroll to Vehicle Tools to find Fuel Type and Ethanol Calculator.

Saved fuel details can make blend planning faster. Review your own saved values before using them. Read E85 and fuel tools for calculator guidance.

Control what appears on your profile

From Home, open your avatar sidebar, then Settings → Privacy & Safety. In Visible on profile, review Show full name, Show city, Show primary vehicle, Show social links, and Show attendance publicly.

Separate profile-access choices include Everyone, Connections only, and Hidden. Choose the settings appropriate for what you want others to see.

Save your profile name

Open Profile → Edit, change Name, and tap Save. Return to your profile to check the name. Your display name and username are separate fields.

Add a modification

Open Garage → Open → your vehicle → Build & Performance → Manage → Add Modification. Enter the record details, review Currently installed, and choose Save.

Enter the modification name and any details you want to keep. Set Currently installed to match whether the part is on your vehicle. Add a photo or installation date if useful.

Check the record in your Build list

After saving, return to Build and check that the new modification is listed.

Adding a modification does not add a power figure. Use Add Power Figure separately if you want to record one.

Review a modification

Open the modification to review its details and installation status. Choose Save for changes or Cancel to leave it as it was.

Check that the category and installation status show what you intended before saving.

Move around your profile

Tap or swipe between Pulse, Garage, and Groups. Use Pulse filters to narrow profile posts, and tap Attended, Hosted, or Connections to open that history or list. Each tab keeps its own place as you move between them.

A joined month and year, when available, indicates when the Idle profile was created. It does not indicate verification or a paid membership.

Troubleshooting

  • Wrong primary vehicle appearsOpen Profile > Garage > Open and tap Make primary beside the vehicle you want to show. The chosen vehicle is marked Primary.
  • Fuel settings look wrongOpen the vehicle and review the saved values before blend planning.
  • Build details are missingOpen the vehicle again. If Build details unavailable appears, tap Try Again. You can also pull down in My Garage to refresh your vehicles.
  • Style option is lockedRestore purchases or choose a style included with your current account.
  • Location still appearsReview Show city in Privacy & Safety, refresh the profile, and contact support if it still appears where it should be hidden.
  • Garage photo did not updateReopen the vehicle editor, choose the photo again and finish the photo editor before tapping Save. Check your connection. If Upload on cellular is off in Settings > Media & Data, connect to Wi-Fi or review that setting.
  • Vehicle missing from the listSend the year, make, model, and trim through Requests.

Privacy

Review your Idle privacy settings if you want to control what profile details are visible. You can also read the Privacy Policy.
