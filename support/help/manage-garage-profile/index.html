Profile

Manage your garage and profile

Your profile and garage help other people understand who you are, what you drive, and how you connect with the scene.

Garage basics

  • Add your vehicleAdd cars or bikes with year, make, model, trim, and photos when available.
  • Choose a primary vehicleYour primary badge can appear around the app next to your name.
  • Use clean photosA clear vehicle photo works better than a cropped or dark image.
  • Add plate blurIf you use plate blur, position the blur over the plate before saving the garage photo.

Profile basics

Keep your username, home base, avatar, banner, bio, groups, and social links current. Your profile is part of how people decide whether to connect.

Troubleshooting

  • Wrong badge appearsCheck which vehicle is marked as primary.
  • Photo did not updateConfirm Photos permission is enabled, then try again.
  • Vehicle missing from the listSend the year, make, model, and trim through Requests.

Privacy

Review your Idle privacy settings if you want to control what profile details are visible. You can also read the Privacy Policy.
