Profile

Manage your garage and profile

Your profile and garage help other people understand who you are, what you drive, and how you connect with the scene. Vehicle detail can also hold Build & Performance information and useful fuel settings, while profile privacy and style controls help you choose how you appear in Idle.

Garage basics

  • Add your vehicleAdd cars or bikes with year, make, model, trim, and photos when available.
  • Choose a primary vehicleYour primary vehicle can appear around the app next to your name.
  • Document your buildWhere available, use Build & Performance to add modifications, owner-reported power, performance results, and optional photo proof.
  • Save fuel detailsAdd fuel type, tank size, pump gas, ethanol defaults, and notes for your own build when useful.
  • Use clean photosA clear vehicle photo works better than a cropped or dark image.
  • Add plate blurIf you use plate blur, position the blur over the plate before saving the garage photo.

Build & Performance

Build & Performance is an enthusiast record for your vehicle. Power and performance figures are entered by the vehicle owner and are not verified by Idle. Only add proof photos you are comfortable attaching to that build.

Fuel tools

Saved fuel details can make blend planning faster when using Idle's ethanol calculator. For E85 search and calculator help, read How E85 and fuel tools work.

Profile basics

Keep your username, home base, avatar, banner, bio, groups, and social links current. Your profile is part of how people decide whether to connect.

Profile style and memberships

Profile style options live in Edit Profile. Some badges, profile photo rings, and profile card decor options may require an eligible Idle membership. Purchases and restores happen through Apple.

Profile location privacy

Use Privacy and Safety settings to choose whether your profile location appears. If you hide it, profile rows around Idle should avoid showing that location publicly.

Troubleshooting

  • Wrong primary vehicle appearsCheck which vehicle is marked as primary.
  • Fuel settings look wrongOpen the vehicle detail and review the saved values before using them for blend planning.
  • Build details are missingOpen the vehicle again and refresh your garage. Build & Performance may not be available on every version of Idle.
  • Style option is lockedRestore purchases or choose a profile style option included with your current account.
  • Location still appearsReview Privacy and Safety settings, refresh the profile, and contact support if the location still appears where it should be hidden.
  • Photo did not updateConfirm Photos permission is enabled, then try again.
  • Vehicle missing from the listSend the year, make, model, and trim through Requests.

Privacy

Review your Idle privacy settings if you want to control what profile details are visible. You can also read the Privacy Policy.
