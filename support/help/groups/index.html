Groups

How groups work

Groups are spaces for crews and communities. They can include group identity, members, discussion, quick chat, mentions, notification preferences, private requests, invites, and group-only meets.

Common actions

  • Discover groupsBrowse groups you can see and request access when needed.
  • Join or requestPublic groups can be joined directly; private groups may require approval.
  • Use discussion and chatDiscussion is for longer updates, while chat is for quick coordination.
  • Share photosOpen group chat photos full screen, zoom in, save them to Photos, or swipe vertically to return when not zoomed.
  • Retry a sendIf a group chat message fails, tap Retry to try the same message again.
  • Mention membersUse group chat mentions when a message needs a current group member's attention.
  • Tune alertsReview notification preferences for a group when you want more or fewer alerts from that group.
  • Manage a groupOwners and admins may have tools for requests, invites, roles, logos, and details.

What to expect

  • Public and private groupsPublic groups can be discoverable. Private groups may require a request or invite before you can see the full group.
  • Group accessGroup-only meets and group chat should only be visible to people with the right group access.
  • Conversation recoveryAn open group chat refreshes recent messages after a temporary connection loss.
  • Reduce MotionFull-screen photo transitions follow your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting.
  • RolesGroup roles can affect who can review requests, invite members, edit details, or manage group content.
  • NotificationsGroup notification preferences work together with your overall Idle and iPhone notification settings.
  • MentionsGroup chat mentions can create Activity or push alerts depending on the member's settings.

Troubleshooting

  • Group missingCheck whether the group is public, private, or requires an invite.
  • Cannot invite someoneThe person may already be a member, invited, requesting access, or not eligible for that invite.
  • Group alerts are too noisyOpen the group's notification settings and tune the categories you want.
  • Group message failedCheck your connection and tap Retry. If a photo is no longer available, choose it again.
  • Photo will not saveCheck Idle's Photos permission in iPhone Settings, then try again.
  • Mention did not notify someoneConfirm the person is still a group member and that their group, Idle, and iPhone notification settings allow alerts.
  • Cannot edit detailsConfirm you are the owner or have the right group role.

Contact support

Email support@idlemeets.com with the group name, your username, and what action is not working.
