Overview

What is Idle?

Idle is an iPhone app for finding and hosting car meets. It also includes garage profiles, E85 and photo-spot discovery, Pulse, groups, messages, and temporary Convoys for active drives.

Main parts of Idle

These screenshots use sample content in a preview version of Idle. Your app may look different. Select an image to see the full screen.

  • Home and SearchHome brings together meets, Pulse updates, host context, and weather. Search includes discovery entry points for E85 stops and photo spots.
  • HostingCreate meets with clear details, flyers, private reusable templates, parking, ride-outs, and Host Tools.
  • Profile & garageMove between your Pulse, garage, and groups, then show your driver identity, vehicles, socials, profile style, and meet history.
  • Connections & messagesConnect with drivers you know and message accepted connections.
  • Groups, Pulse, and ConvoysKeep crews organized, share quick updates, and coordinate active drives.

Idle language

Idle says meets, connections, Activity, Host Tools, garage, groups, Pulse, and Convoys. These words match the app.

What to expect

Idle is built for iPhone. Some features depend on your account state, iPhone permissions, device support, and whether the meet, group, or profile is visible to you.

Need help?

For account-specific, deletion, safety, or private issues, email support@idlemeets.com.
