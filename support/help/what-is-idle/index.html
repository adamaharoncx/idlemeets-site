Overview

What is Idle?

Idle is an iPhone app for car culture. It helps drivers discover meets, find E85 stops and photo spots, host cleaner plans, build a garage/profile, use Pulse, join groups, message connections, and coordinate active drives with Convoys.

Main parts of Idle

  • Home and SearchFind meets, Pulse updates, host context, weather, E85 stops, photo spots, and discovery entry points.
  • HostingCreate meets with clear details, flyers, parking, ride-outs, and Host Tools.
  • Profile & garageShow your driver identity, cars, bikes, photos, fuel details, socials, groups, and meet history.
  • Connections & messagesConnect with drivers you know and message accepted connections.
  • Groups, Pulse, and ConvoysKeep crews organized, share quick updates, and coordinate active drives.

Idle language

Idle says meets, connections, Activity, Host Tools, garage, groups, Pulse, and Convoys. These words match the app.

What to expect

Idle is built for iPhone. Some features depend on your account state, iPhone permissions, device support, and whether the meet, group, or profile is visible to you.

Need help?

For account-specific, deletion, safety, or private issues, email support@idlemeets.com.
